How a chain's effect reaches recorded data

Public datasets come from one chain. To score another, a bridge measured on a MEArec simulation with ground truth carries its effect onto the recorded features: crossings are thinned by the share of real spikes the cheaper chain keeps, and SBP gets the extra noise power. The bridge was checked at {{bridge_ua}} µA, where the cheaper chain can be simulated directly: crossings correlate r = {{bridge_cross_actual}} with true spike counts (bridge predicts {{bridge_cross_predicted}}), SBP {{bridge_sbp_actual}} (bridge predicts {{bridge_sbp_predicted}}).

U5 · Recording while stimulating

A millivolt artifact on a microvolt signal

A {{pulse_ua}} µA, {{pulse_us}} µs biphasic pulse at {{pulse_rate}} pulses per second puts a median {{artifact_mv}} mV ({{artifact_min_mv}} to {{artifact_max_mv}}) on every electrode of its own array, past the ADC's ±{{adc_range_mv}} mV on all of them. The selected chain's front end recovers within ±{{recovery_threshold_uv}} µV in {{recovery_low_ms}} ms (conventional: {{recovery_conv_ms}} ms). Blanking then drops the affected samples, or a multiplier-free adaptive template canceller subtracts a learned artifact for {{canceller_uw}} µW and {{canceller_bits}} bits per channel.

U6 · Aging stress test

Decoding through an aging electrode

Encapsulation raises |Z| at 1 kHz from {{z_fresh_kohm}} to {{z_aged_kohm}} kΩ ({{impedance_rise}} times, within two weeks) and front-end noise from {{noise_fresh_uv}} to {{noise_aged_uv}} µV, while spike amplitude falls about {{decline_per_month}} a month. Decoders fitted at week 0 are tested on held-out blocks aged by the model. A running z-score ({{stabiliser_uw}} µW, {{stabiliser_bits}} register bits per channel) recovers most of the loss without refitting. U0 to U2 · Groundwork

The measurements the choice rests on

  • Real implant noise. {{u0_clips}} clean Neuralink clips: median spike-band noise {{u0_sigma_lsb}} LSB, and {{u0_lfp_share}} of the power sits below 300 Hz. U0
  • Front-end noise three ways. Hand calculation, analytic model and ngspice give {{u1_noise_hand}}, {{u1_noise_analytic}} and {{u1_noise_spice}} µV rms (300 Hz to 7.5 kHz, referred to tissue), within {{u1_disagreement}} of each other. Electrode {{u1_z_kohm}} kΩ at 1 kHz with a constant-phase double layer; NEF {{u1_nef}}. U1
  • How few ADC bits. On a MEArec ground-truth recording at {{u2_noise_uv}} µV background noise, spike-detection F1 holds at {{u2_f1_10b}} at 10 bits and {{u2_f1_6b}} at 6, then drops to {{u2_f1_5b}} at 5. U2
  • No decoder survives the session gap untouched. A fixed ridge readout across NoMAD's sessions scores R² {{fixed_cross_a}} and {{fixed_cross_b}}, which is why aging gets its own unit.

For other researchers

Reproduce, reuse, check

Each figure and number comes from one make target with a fixed seed. The datasets are public, download with SHA-256 checks and are never committed.

Run it

git clone {{repo_url}}
cd neurochain
make setup        # uv sync + pre-commit
make data         # download and verify
make u3           # Pareto sweep, ~4 min
make u3-key ARGS="--nef 2"
make site         # this page

Datasets

  • Nason et al., SBP data, Deep Blue, CC BY-NC 4.0: 96 channels, finger kinematics
  • NoMAD sessions, Dryad: 192 channels, two sessions {{days_apart}} days apart
  • Neuralink compression challenge: 743 raw N1 clips in ADC LSB, no published gain
  • MEArec: simulated ground truth, generated locally

Pieces you can lift

  • neurochain.frontend: Randles electrode with a constant-phase element, amplifier noise model, causal front-end emulator, ngspice netlist generator
  • neurochain.power: per-stage µW model for amplifier, ADC, logic and radio
  • neurochain.bridge: carries a cheaper chain's effect onto recorded features
  • neurochain.stim: artifact propagation, saturation model and fixed-point template canceller
  • neurochain.stabilise: running z-score with shift-only arithmetic
  • rtl/: SystemVerilog chain with a Verilator bit-exactness harness

What this does not show

  • No hardware yet. The board, saline phantom and stimulator are the next unit.
  • Power constants and aging parameters are assumptions from the literature. They are varied, not measured.
  • Most chains are scored through the bridge, not recorded directly.
  • NoMAD's two sessions are the only measured long-term gap.
